The Aviation Carbon-Emission Network of the Yangtze River Delta

Intercity aviation carbon emissions from Yangtze River Delta (YRD) related passenger flights, 2019–2023, drawn as a network across China. Each circle is a city; lines are the busiest routes by emissions. Choose a year and a lens: total emissions, hub role in the network, or the community a city belongs to. Click a city to trace its routes. Emissions are concentrated in a few hubs, carried by trunk routes, and structurally persistent through the COVID-19 shock of 2022.

Circle size scales with the chosen measure; a dark ring marks the 22 YRD airport cities. Lines are intercity routes, thicker for more route emissions. Hover for values; click a city to isolate its routes, click empty sea to reset.
