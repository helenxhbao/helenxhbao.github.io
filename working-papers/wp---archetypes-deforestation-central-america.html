Archetypes and Drivers of Deforestation in Central America's Tropical Moist Forests

Abstract

Deforestation assessments still treat tropical moist forest loss as a uniform process, and do not distinguish the frontier dynamics that shape where and how forests are lost and the drivers associated with them. Here, we develop a regional spatially explicit frontier classification for Central America’s tropical moist forest by combining annual 30-m forest-change data (1990-2023) with a frontier-metrics framework aggregated to 1.5-km cells.
