The ESG Facade: Disclosure and Lip Service among Chinese Listed Firms

Geographic and industry patterns of ESG disclosure and lip-service (LS) behaviour among sampled Chinese listed firms, 2006–2022. Lip service identifies firms that disclose extensively on ESG yet show comparatively weak ESG performance. Explore by year, province, and ESG dimension; hover a province for its rate. The industries most engaged in each behaviour are ranked in the panel. Provinces with fewer than five sampled firms in a year are left grey.

Colour = the share of that province's sampled firms exhibiting the selected behaviour. Hover a province for the rate and firm count. The South China Sea islands and the ten-dash line are shown in the corner inset.
