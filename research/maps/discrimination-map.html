The New Geography of Housing Discrimination

The New Geography of Housing Discrimination

Field-experiment estimates of minority disadvantage in housing search, drawn from the studies reviewed in the Race, symbolic stigma and foreignness project. An odds ratio below 1 means the minority applicant was less likely to get a positive response than the majority reference: below 1 is disadvantage, above 1 is advantage. Use the filters to read the map by year, ethnic group, applicant gender, and study stage; the blue dots mark on-going studies.
