The ESG Facade: Disclosure and Lip Service among Chinese Listed Firms

The ESG Facade: Disclosure and Lip Service among Chinese Listed Firms

Geographic and industry patterns of ESG disclosure and lip-service (LS) behaviour among sampled Chinese listed firms, 2006–2022. Lip service identifies firms that disclose extensively on ESG yet show comparatively weak ESG performance. Explore by year, province, industry, and ESG dimension; hover a province for its rate. This accompanies Xu, Liu and Bao, Beyond the ESG Facade: Measuring and Addressing Corporate ‘Lip Service’ (2026, Business Strategy and the Environment).
