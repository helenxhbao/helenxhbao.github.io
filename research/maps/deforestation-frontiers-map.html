Frontier Archetypes of Central America's Tropical Moist Forests

Frontier Archetypes of Central America's Tropical Moist Forests

Where and how tropical moist forest is being lost across Central America, 1990–2023, classified on a 1.5-km grid into frontier archetypes and the three thematic typologies (deforestation severity, spatiotemporal pattern, and development stage) that combine into them. Choose a layer, hover to read a cell, and click a class to isolate it; for the archetypes, a heatmap shows which covariates shift the odds of each frontier state. This accompanies Chicas, Mizoue, Bao and coauthors, Mapping deforestation frontiers in Central America’s tropical moist forests (2026, Communications Earth & Environment).
