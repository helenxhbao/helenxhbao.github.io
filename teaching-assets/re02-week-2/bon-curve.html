Does construction follow a bell curve as economies grow?

The Bon curve says the share of construction in output rises, peaks at middle income, then falls. Try different measures, groups of economies, periods and ways of using the data, and see which settings look most like a bell curve. The page opens on the settings shown on slide 5 (marked “as on slide 5”), the combination that comes closest.

Data: United Nations Statistics Division, National Accounts Main Aggregates Database (construction, ISIC F; GDP; investment; population), and World Bank, World Development Indicators (GDP per head in purchasing-power terms; income groups and regions), 1970 to 2023. Concept: Bon, R. (1992), “The future of international construction: secular patterns of growth and decline”, Habitat International 16(3), 119–128; Ruddock, L. and Lopes, J. (2006), “The construction sector and economic development: the ‘Bon curve’”, Construction Management and Economics 24(7), 717–723; Lewis, T.M.
